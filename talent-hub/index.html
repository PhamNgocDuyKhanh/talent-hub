About us

What we're building

CBTW specializes in the Build–Operate–Transfer model — a long-term way to grow your product roadmap without outsourcing your development. In a few weeks we can stand up a dedicated team in Vietnam that steadily integrates into your organization.

01 · Build

Build

We source candidates from our talent pool based on your needs. You make the final call on team structure and vet every engineer as we build your development center from the ground up.

02 · Operate

Operate

We manage and operate your development center to a high standard, so you can focus on your roadmap and core business instead of day-to-day team operations.

03 · Transfer

Transfer

When you're ready, take full legal ownership of the center — office, equipment, engineers, managers, and support staff — and run it fully autonomously.
